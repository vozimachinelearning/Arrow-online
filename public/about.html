Our Story

From LoOper to Arrow

Arrow (originally developed under the codename "LoOper") was born from a simple observation: the AI and automation landscape was broken. Users were forced to choose between expensive cloud subscriptions, privacy-invasive platforms, and fragile agent loops that derailed on long-running tasks.

We built Arrow to consolidate five separate platforms — RPA tools, AI APIs, schedulers, coding agents, and digital twin platforms — into a single, offline-first, desktop-native application. The result is a platform where automation is reliable, privacy is absolute, and cost is never a barrier.
