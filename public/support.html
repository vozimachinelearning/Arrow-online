Frequently Asked Questions
How much does Arrow cost for individuals? +
Arrow is not free for individuals. It's a flat $10/month — $10 for the first month, then just $2/month after that for ongoing support. Since Arrow does not collect telemetry, a new license key is issued each month to validate your subscription. No per-token charges, no execution limits, no feature gating.
Does Arrow require an internet connection? +
No. Arrow runs 100% offline. All AI inference, automation execution, and license validation happen on your device. The only time internet is needed is for downloading models and updates.
What AI models does Arrow support? +
Arrow supports two inference backends: Ollama (for pull-based model management) and llama.cpp (for direct GGUF model execution). Five models are bundled, including SmolLM3 for routing, LFM for reasoning, and vision models for screen understanding.
Can I use Arrow for commercial purposes? +
The Individual tier is for personal use. Organizations requiring commercial use, 24/7 support, SLA guarantees, and custom integration should contact us about the Enterprise tier.
Does Arrow collect any telemetry? +
Zero. Arrow collects no telemetry, usage data, or analytics. Your data, workflows, and automations remain entirely on your device. Because we don't collect telemetry, a new license key is generated each month to verify your subscription — keeping things simple and private.
How does the license key work? +
Since Arrow does not collect telemetry, we use monthly license keys to verify your subscription instead. Pay $10 for your first month to receive your initial key. Each subsequent month is $2 for support, and a new key is issued automatically. No auto-renewal — you choose to renew each month.
How do I upgrade to Enterprise? +
Contact our sales team through the Contact tab. Enterprise licenses include 24/7 support, custom chains, SLA guarantees, and WAN cluster processing.

Troubleshooting
Arrow won't start after installation +
Ensure your system meets the minimum requirements (Windows 10 22H2+, 8GB RAM, 5GB free space). Try reinstalling the MSI. If the issue persists, check the log files at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Arrow\logs.
Models fail to download +
Ensure you have a stable internet connection for the initial download. Models are downloaded from Hugging Face. If downloads fail, restart Arrow — it will resume where it left off.
License validation errors +
Arrow uses hardware-bound license validation. If your hardware changes significantly, you may need to reinstall your license. Contact support for assistance with license recovery.
